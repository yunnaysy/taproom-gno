How it works

      

        
1
Pin
A poster calls PostQuest with a title, details, a window and at least 1 GNOT attached. The reward is locked in the realm.

        
2
Answer
Each hunter sends one answer with a note and an optional https link while the window is open.

        
3
Award
The poster picks a winner and the escrow is paid in the same transaction.

        
4
Reclaim
No answers: withdraw any time. With answers: blocked until the deadline plus a 7-day review window, after which the poster can reclaim.
